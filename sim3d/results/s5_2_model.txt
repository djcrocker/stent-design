Superelastic card bound to all 8128 elements of spikeA_sector; 0.2% axial stretch.
Peak von Mises 66.5 MPa, so the response is elastic and hand-checkable.

66.5 MPa is ~0.51x the nominal E*strain = 130 MPa. Expected: a cellular
structure accommodates global strain by strut bending rather than stretching,
so local material strain sits below the applied global strain. Implied attenuation
~2.0x, consistent with the 1.49-2.09x the 2D screen measures (sim2d/fatigue.py).

Note that the sector cut faces are free in this run: no cyclic symmetry constraints
are applied yet. This is fine for proving the material binds; must be added or the
model is a free strip rather than 1/12 of a ring.
